🔄 Alpha Reinvestment

When accumulated cash alpha can buy ≥ 1 lot — defined as 1 share Asset1 + round(Y/X) shares Asset2 at the current pool ratio — those shares are purchased and added to the pool. L is recomputed from the new total holdings, compounding fee generation on every future swap.

① Cash alpha accumulates from profitable AMM swaps
② Lot = 1 share A + round(Y/X) shares B at current ratio.
Example: pool 50 : 150 → lot = 1 A + 3 B
③ N = floor(cash / lot cost inc. brok). All N lots bought atomically.
④ xShares += N, yShares += N × round(Y/X). Hold benchmark expands symmetrically — IL invariant preserved at reinvestment instant.
⑤ L recomputed from new pool capital → higher future fee harvest. Compounds indefinitely while swaps generate alpha.
Accounting note: The hold benchmark (xHold, yHold) grows with each reinvestment, so IL reflects only AMM-induced divergence, not reinvestment timing. vsHold = totalValue − holdValue is always apples-to-apples.
