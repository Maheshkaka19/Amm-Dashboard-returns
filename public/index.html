⟡ Calculus Range Engine

LAGRANGIAN OPTIMISATION α* = Newton-Raphson on:
f(α) = 2√(1−α)/(2−α) − 1 + θ = 0
where θ = IL budget (adaptive)

CONCENTRATION FACTOR C(α) = 1 / (1 − √(rLow/rHigh))

ADAPTIVE BUDGET θ_eff = θ_base · exp(−λ · cashROI%)
IL Budget θ (%) Max acceptable IL% → solver sets concentration range. Tighter = higher fees & IL risk. Looser = safer but lower yield.
Protect Cap (%) CashROI% at which budget → minimum
Decay λ Speed of budget shrinkage
Volatility Width Multiplier Widens range during volatile hours (EWMA σ). Higher = fewer recenters at cost of lower concentration.
How the engine works:
Every hour, the solver computes α* — the exact half-width that maximises concentration while keeping worst-case IL within the budget. Inventory skew asymmetrically widens the side where rebalancing is needed. As alpha builds, θ_eff shrinks automatically to protect gains.
